3. What is hoisting in JavaScript? 
--->   hoisting is the JavaScript behaviour of moving declearation to the top of their scope before execution 
        Variable Hoisting
            console.log(x);
            var x = 10;

        Internally:
        var x;
        console.log(x); // undefined
        x = 10;

    Important
var → hoisted and initialized with undefined
let and const → hoisted but remain in the Temporal Dead Zone (TDZ)
